# Community conduct

Treat participants respectfully regardless of background, experience, disability or identity. Do not demand diagnostic disclosure. Constructive technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, doxxing and discriminatory behavior are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, and restrict participation proportionately. For sensitive reports, request a private maintainer channel rather than publishing personal details. The project must establish a monitored contact channel before advertising a guaranteed response process. This is a project-specific conduct policy, not an attributed copy of another standard.
